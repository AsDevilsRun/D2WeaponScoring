D2 Vault Cleaner

Scores every legendary weapon you own against your Tiered Weapons tab and lists the copies that are junk. Same rules as your Scores tab: a copy is junk if another copy of that weapon scores higher, or if the weapon isn't on your list.

Setup

This page needs its own Bungie app. At bungie.net/en/Application, create one with OAuth client type Public, the redirect URL set to this page's address, the origin header set to this site's origin, and the scope Read your Destiny 2 information. Then put its API key and OAuth client ID in CONFIG at the top of the script.
